# Totem — Business Plan (Simple Version)

A plain-English Q&A of the strategy. If you want the full reasoning, see `business.md`.

---

## Q: What is Totem, in one sentence?

**A:** Totem is an AI-powered ad agency for AI/ML infrastructure companies. We simulate their customers, make lots of ads, and help them turn sign-ups into real enterprise sales.

---

## Q: Who do we sell to? (Vertical)

**A:** AI/ML infrastructure companies making $5–30M/year. Think: Langfuse, Braintrust, Helicone, Baseten, Modal, Runpod, Langsmith, Weights & Biases.

---

## Q: Why this vertical?

**A:** Six reasons:

1. **They have money.** Braintrust raised $80M. Langfuse, Modal, Baseten all raised big. Boards are pushing them to spend on growth.
2. **Their current ads are bad.** Boring screenshots and headshots on LinkedIn. Easy to beat.
3. **No gatekeeper.** Founders and small marketing teams make decisions — no CMO, no committee, no procurement.
4. **Our pitch actually fits.** Their customers (AI engineers, ML leads) are very specific. "Simulate your customer" hits harder here than anywhere.
5. **No legal risk.** No HIPAA, no banking rules. AI-generated visuals of "dashboards" are fine.
6. **Founders talk to each other.** YC, a16z, Latent Space — one happy client brings three more.

---

## Q: Why NOT small local businesses (HVAC, flooring)?

**A:** Because running two agencies with two founders is impossible. SMB clients don't care about our thesis — they just want cheap leads. And you can't be "the AI agency" and "the flooring agency" at the same time without confusing everyone.

## Q: How much do we charge?

**A:** For the first client: **$4,000/month base + $600 per qualified lead**. 90-day pilot. If we don't deliver 4 leads in month 1, they pay nothing.

Later clients get different models:
- **Client 2:** $12K/month flat for 40 ads/month.
- **Client 3:** $5K/month + 4% of pipeline we generate (capped at $30K/month).

---

## Q: Why these prices?

**A:** $25–50K total pilot fits under the $50K threshold where VPs can sign without procurement getting involved. That's the single biggest factor in closing deals fast.

---

## Q: What do we actually deliver?

**A:** Every month, per client:
- **40+ ads** across 3 customer types × 3–4 formats.
- A customer simulation report (our unique thing).
- A media plan (where to spend the money).
- Weekly performance reports.
- Monthly optimization memo.

For comparison: normal agencies make 4–8 ads/month. We make 40+. That's the whole value prop.

---

## Q: Where should the ads run?

**A:** A portfolio, not one channel. For a $20–150K/month client budget:

- **70% core demand:** Google Search, LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube
- **20% brand-building:** newsletters (TLDR, Pragmatic Engineer), podcasts (Lenny's, Acquired), connected TV
- **10% developer-specific:** Reddit, EthicalAds, Stack Overflow

**Why not just Meta?** Meta is cheap but only reaches the 5% of buyers actively shopping. The other 95% need brand touchpoints for when they *become* ready to buy.

---

## Q: What kinds of ads do we make?

**A:** Three lanes:

1. **Polished LinkedIn ads** — clean, professional, big logo, specific headline.
2. **"Ugly" UGC-style ads** — phone footage, founder face, casual. For Meta and Reddit. These get 4x higher CTR than polished ads on these platforms.
3. **Thought Leader posts** — written in the founder's voice, posted from their personal LinkedIn. Highest-performing format in B2B right now (6x more clicks than normal ads).

---

## Q: Who competes with us?

**A:** No one does the full thing (simulate → create → deploy → optimize). The biggest threats:

- **Omneky** — closest product match, but weak in dev tools.
- **Mutiny** — $71M raised, one announcement away from being us.
- **Aaru** — $1B valuation, owns "synthetic audiences" in people's heads.

Traditional agencies (NoGood, Kalungi, Refine Labs) charge $5–45K/month but can't scale creative output. That's our opening.

---

## Q: How do we actually sell?

**A:** Six steps:

1. **Do the work first.** Build 12 spec ads for the target company *before* reaching out. "Hey, I already made you these — want to see?"
2. **DM the founder on LinkedIn.** Not email. Include a 60-second Loom.
3. **Short discovery call.** 25 minutes, not 60. Show the spec ads.
4. **One-page pilot contract.** No 40-page MSA for a pilot.
5. **Execute 90-day pilot.** Weekly updates, obsessive tracking.
6. **Convert at day 75** to a 12-month contract at 3–5x the pilot fee.

## Q: What are the biggest risks?

**A:**

1. **Developers reject AI ads as "slop."** Fix: ship at Linear/Stripe quality bar only.
2. **Mutiny or Aaru copies us.** Fix: move fast, publish weekly, ship case studies.
3. **Founders think they don't need us.** Fix: pitch as "we turn your ideas into 40 ads/week," never as replacement.
4. **We run out of money.** Fix: $25K pilot = $25K cash immediately. No hires until $50K MRR.

---

## Q: What do we NOT do?

**A:**

- No percentage-of-ad-spend pricing (bad incentives).
- No regulated industries (pharma, banking, crypto) in year 1.
- No AI-generated human faces in testimonials (FTC risk).
- No fake product screenshots. Real UI only; AI for backgrounds/textures.
- No equity deals. Feels desperate.
- No annual contracts before a pilot.
