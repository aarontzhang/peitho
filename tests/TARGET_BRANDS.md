# Target Brands for Creative Testing

**Purpose:** Test Peitho's creative pipeline across brands with strong, distinct identities. Validate that we can replicate real brand voice/visual DNA, generate persona-specific variants, and produce LinkedIn-ready assets — not just posters.

---

## What We've Done

**Nike — Female Athletes 2026**
- 5 ICP segments (Collegiate, Weekend Warrior, Gen-Z Trailblazer, Elite Aspirant, Comeback)
- 5 persona-specific posters (1080x1350, Instagram/Digital OOH)
- Pipeline: config → briefs → Gemini image gen → Puppeteer compositing

**What we learned:** The poster pipeline works. Persona psychology drives genuinely different creative angles. Now we need to test breadth (more brands) and format diversity (LinkedIn-native assets, not just posters).

---

## Asset Types to Generate

### 1. LinkedIn Single Image Ad (1080x1080)
- Square format, takes max feed space
- Bold text overlay on image, not stock photography
- 150-char intro text (above image, before truncation)
- 70-char headline (below image)
- CTA button
- **This is the workhorse B2B format — lowest CPM, most reliable**

### 2. LinkedIn Carousel / Document Post (1080x1350 per slide)
- 4:5 portrait, 5-10 slides
- Highest organic reach format on LinkedIn right now
- Great for "here's the strategic thinking behind this creative" breakdowns
- Each slide: bold headline + supporting visual/text
- **Best for Peitho's own LinkedIn presence — show the work, show the process**

### 3. LinkedIn Post Copy (Text + Image)
- Hook in first 2 lines (pattern interrupt, contrarian take, specific number)
- 800-1,200 chars optimal for personal posts
- Line breaks every 1-2 sentences
- End with question/CTA to drive comments
- **Pair with single image or carousel**

### 4. Poster / Display (1080x1350)
- What we already have from the Nike test
- Instagram / Digital OOH format
- Good for visual portfolio pieces

## Brands to Test

### Tier 1: Consumer Brands with Iconic Identity
These are high-visual-impact brands where recreating the brand feel proves the pipeline works. Great for portfolio pieces and LinkedIn showcase content.

#### 1. Apple
- **Visual DNA:** Minimalist white space, San Francisco typeface, product-as-hero, clean gradients
- **Voice:** Simple declarative sentences. One feature, one line. "Shot on iPhone." Understated confidence.
- **Why test:** The opposite of Nike — restraint instead of intensity. Tests whether the pipeline can do quiet precision, not just bold aggression.
- **Product to feature:** MacBook Pro or AirPods Pro
- **Suggested ICPs:** Creative professional, Enterprise IT buyer, Student/educator, Developer

#### 2. Patagonia
- **Visual DNA:** Natural landscapes, earth tones, documentary photography, worn-in texture
- **Voice:** Activist, contrarian. "Don't Buy This Jacket." Environmental urgency wrapped in brand storytelling. Long-form copy that actually says something.
- **Why test:** Brand voice is values-driven, not product-driven. Tests whether the pipeline can do purpose-led creative where the product is secondary.
- **Product to feature:** Nano Puff jacket or Baggies shorts
- **Suggested ICPs:** Outdoor enthusiast/climber, Eco-conscious professional, Weekend hiker/casual buyer, Corporate sustainability lead

#### 3. Liquid Death
- **Visual DNA:** Skull imagery, metal/punk aesthetic, tall-can format, dark humor, vintage horror illustration
- **Voice:** Irreverent, absurd, anti-marketing. "Murder your thirst." Deliberately provocative. Makes fun of its own category.
- **Why test:** Extreme brand personality. Tests whether the pipeline can capture voice that's genuinely weird and not sanitize it into generic "edgy" copy.
- **Product to feature:** Mountain Water or Severed Lime
- **Suggested ICPs:** Gen-Z health-conscious contrarian, Sober-curious millennial, Festival/music scene, Irony-pilled office worker

#### 4. Spotify
- **Visual DNA:** Bold duotone gradients, data-as-creative (Wrapped), heavy typography, vibrant color blocking
- **Voice:** Playful, culturally fluent, data-informed. "Thanks to the 3,445 people who streamed 'It's the End of the World as We Know It' the day of the Brexit vote."
- **Why test:** Data-driven creative is unique. Tests whether the pipeline can generate insight-first creative where the hook is a stat, not a tagline.
- **Product to feature:** Spotify Premium or Spotify for Podcasters
- **Suggested ICPs:** Music-obsessed Gen-Z, Commuter/productivity listener, Independent artist/creator, Marketing professional (B2B angle for ad platform)

#### 5. Oatly
- **Visual DNA:** Hand-drawn typography, packaging-as-billboard, deliberately rough illustration, off-white/natural palette
- **Voice:** Self-aware, chatty, breaks the fourth wall. "This tastes like sh*t! — No one." Long copy that reads like a conversation. Anti-polish.
- **Why test:** The most distinctive copy voice in CPG. Tests whether AI can replicate genuine personality vs. falling into generic "quirky brand" territory.
- **Product to feature:** Oat Milk (original) or Oatly Barista Edition
- **Suggested ICPs:** Environmentally motivated switcher, Coffee snob/barista, Lactose-intolerant pragmatist, Skeptical "just try it" prospect

#### 6. Arc'teryx
- **Visual DNA:** Technical product photography, mountain environments, muted earth/grey palette, clean Swiss-influenced typography
- **Voice:** Precise, technical, understated. "Designed for the ascent." No hype. Performance specs delivered with quiet authority.
- **Why test:** Premium positioning through restraint. Tests whether the pipeline can convey luxury/technical credibility without overselling.
- **Product to feature:** Alpha SV Jacket or Beta LT
- **Suggested ICPs:** Alpine climber/mountaineer, Urban techwear buyer, Gear researcher/spec-obsessed, Investment-piece shopper

### Tier 2: B2B Brands
Closer to Peitho's actual market. These tests prove we can do the work we're actually selling — B2B persona-specific creative.

#### 7. Slack
- **Visual DNA:** Bright multi-color palette (purple, green, yellow, red), playful illustration, clean UI screenshots, friendly geometry
- **Voice:** Casual, warm, slightly playful. "Where work happens." Makes enterprise software feel human. Uses real workplace scenarios.
- **Why test:** B2B brand that doesn't feel B2B. Tests whether we can generate creative that's professional but not corporate.
- **Product to feature:** Slack for Enterprise or Slack AI
- **Suggested ICPs:** VP of Engineering (team productivity), Head of Remote/Hybrid Work, IT Admin (security/compliance), Project Manager (cross-team coordination)

#### 8. Stripe
- **Visual DNA:** Rich gradients (purple/blue/teal), clean code snippets as visual elements, dark mode aesthetic, precision typography
- **Voice:** Developer-first, technically credible, premium. "Payments infrastructure for the internet." No buzzwords. Explains complex things simply.
- **Why test:** Developer-audience B2B is notoriously hard to market to. Tests whether persona psychology works for technical buyers who hate marketing.
- **Product to feature:** Stripe Payments or Stripe Atlas
- **Suggested ICPs:** Startup CTO (build vs. buy), Engineering Manager (integration complexity), CFO (payment operations), Solo founder (getting to revenue fast)

#### 9. HubSpot
- **Visual DNA:** Orange/coral palette, friendly illustration style, educational content layouts, clean data visualization
- **Voice:** Helpful, educational, approachable. "Grow better." Teaches before it sells. Content marketing DNA — the brand IS the content.
- **Why test:** Content-first B2B brand. Tests whether the pipeline can generate educational-angle creative that builds authority, not just awareness.
- **Product to feature:** HubSpot CRM or Marketing Hub
- **Suggested ICPs:** Head of Marketing (scaling without headcount), Sales Manager (pipeline visibility), Startup founder (first CRM decision), Marketing ops (consolidating tools)

#### 10. Notion
- **Visual DNA:** Clean minimalism, black/white with subtle warm accents, line-art icons, generous whitespace, system-font aesthetic
- **Voice:** Calm, clear, modular. "Write. Plan. Organize." Everything is a building block. Empowers without prescribing.
- **Why test:** Minimal brand expression with strong cult following. Tests whether the pipeline can create desire for a tool through showing the output, not the product.
- **Product to feature:** Notion AI or Notion for Teams
- **Suggested ICPs:** Product Manager (centralized docs), Engineering team lead (wiki replacement), Freelancer/creator (second brain), Operations lead (process standardization)

### Tier 3: Stretch / High-Impact
These are ambitious tests — luxury, heritage, or category-defining brands where nailing the tone is hard. Good for showing range.

#### 11. Porsche
- **Visual DNA:** Cinematic automotive photography, deep blacks and metallic highlights, heritage racing imagery, Porsche typeface
- **Voice:** Engineering heritage. "There is no substitute." Performance data delivered with emotional weight. German precision meets driver passion.
- **Why test:** Luxury brand where the psychology of aspiration vs. ownership is well-studied. Perfect test case for persona-driven creative.
- **Product to feature:** 911 or Taycan
- **Suggested ICPs:** Achievement-driven executive, Enthusiast/track day driver, Tech-forward early adopter (Taycan), Heritage collector

#### 12. Figma
- **Visual DNA:** Bright multicolor, cursor-as-design-element, collaborative multiplayer imagery, design artifacts as visual language
- **Voice:** Community-first, collaborative, creative empowerment. "Nothing great is made alone." Celebrates the process, not just the output.
- **Why test:** Design tool marketing to designers — the most visually critical audience. If the pipeline output looks generic, this audience will reject it immediately.
- **Product to feature:** Figma for Design Teams or Dev Mode
- **Suggested ICPs:** Design lead (scaling design systems), Frontend developer (design-to-code handoff), Product Manager (cross-functional alignment), Solo designer (portfolio to production)

## Priority Order for Testing

The B2B brands are the real test. Consumer brands can lean on cinematic photography — athletes, mountains, products. B2B has to make abstract concepts (payments infrastructure, CRM, team messaging) visually compelling with typography, illustration, and layout. That's the harder problem, and it's what Peitho's actual clients will need.

| Priority | Brand | Rationale |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | **Stripe** | Developer-audience B2B. Closest to actual Peitho use case. No product photos to lean on — pure concept selling. How do you make "payments infrastructure" feel premium? |
| 2 | **Slack** | B2B that doesn't feel B2B. Tests whether we can generate creative that's warm and human for enterprise software. Abstract value prop (team communication). |
| 3 | **HubSpot** | Educational B2B, content-first brand. Tests generating teach-before-you-sell creative. Direct comparison possible — their actual LinkedIn ads are everywhere. |
| 4 | **Notion** | Minimalist cult brand selling an abstract tool. The product IS the content inside it. How do you advertise a blank canvas? |
| 5 | **Figma** | Design-literate audience — highest scrutiny. If AI-generated creative for a design tool looks generic, designers will roast it. Hardest audience to fool. |
| 6 | **Liquid Death** | Extreme consumer voice. Proves range — if we can do Stripe AND Liquid Death, the pipeline handles any tone. |
| 7 | **Apple** | Opposite of Nike — restraint as brand expression. Recognizable immediately if we nail it. |
| 8 | **Patagonia** | Values-driven creative where the product is secondary. Tests purpose-led messaging. |
| 9 | **Oatly** | Copy voice challenge — most distinctive voice in CPG. If the AI can write like Oatly, it can write. |
| 10 | **Spotify** | Data-as-creative. Unique format we haven't tested. |
| 11 | **Arc'teryx** | Premium restraint in outdoor context. |
| 12 | **Porsche** | Luxury aspiration. Hard to do well — proves ceiling. |

## LinkedIn-Specific Notes

**For Peitho's own LinkedIn presence (organic posts):**
- Use carousel/document format (1080x1350 slides) to break down creative work: "Here's the persona psychology behind this Nike ad variant"
- Personal posts from founders get 5-10x the reach of company page posts
- Hook formula: contrarian B2B take → breakdown of our process → CTA/question
- Post Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM target timezone
- First-hour comments are the #1 algorithm signal — engage immediately

**For client-facing ad tests:**
- Single image ads (1080x1080) are the workhorse — start here for each brand
- Bold text overlay on image outperforms stock photography
- 150 chars of intro text is all that shows before truncation — front-load the hook
- Square format for ads, 4:5 portrait for organic

**Format roadmap for each brand test:**
1. Start with 1080x1080 LinkedIn single image ad (fast, comparable)
2. Add LinkedIn post copy (headline + 150-char hook + full post body)
3. Then 1080x1350 poster (extend existing pipeline)
4. Optional: carousel deck breaking down the persona strategy
